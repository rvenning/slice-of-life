❓ How to Play

Customers tell you their order once, out loud. Listen — then make exactly that.

  1. 🛎 Tap the customer at the counter to take their order. Missed it? 🗣 What? says it again, but they get a little less happy.
  2. 🥄 Pick a sauce and drag it over the dough. 🧀 Then cheese — drag over it twice for extra.
  3. 🍄 Tap a topping bin, then tap or drag onto the pizza. Half and half means a different topping on each side.
  4. 🔥 Send it to the oven. One trip is normal, two is well done, three is extra crispy. Four burns it!
  5. 🔪 Swipe across the middle to cut. One cut makes 2 slices, two make 4, three make 6 (the usual) and so on.
  6. 📦 Box it, then hand it over. Wrong order? They hand it back and want their money.

You can have three pizzas on the go at once — tap a ticket under the counter to switch between them. Ingredients cost money, so don't pile it on. The recipe book in The Book tab explains every named pizza.
